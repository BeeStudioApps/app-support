Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains how BeeStudioApps handles information in Beat4 and on this support site. Beat4 is a general-audience game for people 13 and older; it is not directed to children under 13.

Information stored on your device

Beat4 stores gameplay and completion progress, active game state, scores and statistics, game settings, tutorial progress, achievement information, and recovery allowances on your device so the game can work offline. Beat4 does not require an account and does not use a first-party analytics service.

Backups and device transfers

On iOS, Beat4 can use iCloud key-value storage to sync completed-level progress, including best performance information and puzzle-version metadata. On Android, the operating system may back up and restore a dedicated completed-progress record or transfer it to another device, subject to the app’s backup rules and your device and Google backup settings. Active boards, recovery quotas, rewarded-ad credits, purchase entitlements, sound and haptic settings, and account identifiers are excluded from the app’s completed-progress sync and backup payload. Other device-level backups depend on your platform settings. Apple and Google control their backup services and their handling of information stored with them. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising and privacy choices

Beat4 includes Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) support. When advertising is enabled, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share information such as IP address (which may indicate general location), product interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, as described by Google. Where UMP requires a choice, the app can show applicable consent choices and an in-app Ad privacy choices control.

Read Google’s Flutter privacy guidance and Google’s Privacy Policy. You can also manage advertising-related settings through your device and Google account controls where available.

Purchases

If you make or restore an in-app purchase, Apple App Store or Google Play will process the transaction. Beat4 may use purchase status and transaction identifiers on the device to validate a premium entitlement; BeeStudioApps does not receive payment-card details through the app.

Sharing

Core gameplay does not upload local progress to a BeeStudioApps game server. Information may be handled by Apple or Google when you use their platform services, including iCloud, Android backup, App Store or Google Play purchases, or Google advertising if enabled. Their policies govern their independent processing.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you remove it through app or device controls, delete the app, or reset the device, subject to any device backup that you have enabled. Backed-up information remains subject to Apple’s or Google’s backup settings and retention practices. Deleting the app or local data does not by itself request deletion from a platform backup.

Security

Beat4 keeps core gameplay data in your device’s app storage. Completed-progress sync and device backups work as described above and are subject to platform controls. This site is static and does not run analytics or advertising scripts. iCloud, Android backup, app-store purchases, and advertising are handled by their respective platform or provider when used. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Support requests

If you email support, BeeStudioApps and its email provider receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and handle related support work. We keep support email messages and the email addresses associated with them for up to 12 months after an issue is closed, then delete them unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. You can request earlier deletion of this support correspondence by emailing beestudioapps@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

This website

This site is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This site does not use third-party analytics, advertising scripts, or web fonts. See GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests, email beestudioapps@gmail.com. Requests about Apple or Google provider-held information may need to be made through that provider’s privacy controls. We may update this policy when Beat4 or the services it uses change; the effective date above will be updated with the revised policy.
